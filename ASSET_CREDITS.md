# Asset credits and media terms

Ultima V Redux is a free, non-commercial fan project. Its project-authored source
code is MIT-licensed; this does not place every bundled font, sound, image or game
fixture under MIT. Media retains its applicable rights and the terms identified
below. Playing requires a separately obtained original Ultima V installation.

These credits supplement the runtime audio manifest and the notices shipped with
individual assets. Source-page licence labels were checked on 2026-10-10 where
noted. Some recordings have been transcoded or divided into clips; playback windows
and gains are recorded in `internal/audio/assets/library.json`. Attribution does
not itself grant additional rights to the underlying works.

## Original game and project artwork

Original Ultima V material remains credited to Origin Systems and its original
contributors. The game displays the original contributor credits, including the
artwork and music acknowledgments. Original-derived tile sheets, IBM/runic bitmap
font sheets, screenshots and compatibility fixtures are not relicensed as MIT by
their inclusion here. The bitmap sheets are distinct from the FontStruct TTF below.
The application icon shown for the window, the macOS Dock and the Windows
executable is the original Avatar tile (atlas tile 284) from the bundled tile
sheet, enlarged by whole-pixel replication; it carries the same terms as that
sheet.

The Redux logo and blue/white border artwork were created by Brad Hannah. The
harpsichord-note bank was synthesized for the project from the original note
frequencies. Custom prompted effects, including the bard/lute, level-up, apparition,
fountain, horse-gallop, shoal-scrape and potion-drinking sounds, were generated by
Brad Hannah using ElevenLabs Sound Effects during a paid subscription. Their use remains
subject to the applicable [ElevenLabs terms](https://elevenlabs.io/terms).

Brad Hannah also generated the two legacy healing recordings, originally named
`HealingSound.wav` and `HealingSound2.wav`, in Audacity. These are the bundled
`sounds/magic/healing_01.wav` and `sounds/magic/healing_02.wav` files under
`internal/audio/assets/`. They are distinct from the credited third-party healing
MP3s below. No separate media licence has yet been recorded for these two WAVs.

## Music

The 16 MIDI sources came from **The Exodus Project's U5 MIDI Music** package:
[download page](https://exodus.voyd.net/downloads/) and
[MIDI archive](https://github.com/mcmagi/ultima-exodus/releases/download/init/u5midi.zip).
The Exodus Project is maintained by **Michael C. Maggio / Voyager Dragon**; its U5
upgrade supplies edited arrangements of the game score. Brad Hannah converted the
MIDI tracks to the bundled MP3s. This attribution distinguishes arrangement/source
and conversion from original composition; original music retains its applicable rights.

The inspected MIDI package includes a track inventory, but no explicit
redistribution licence was found. Identifying this source and converting the tracks
does not establish additional permission for the arrangements or compositions.

Tracks: Ultima Theme; Britannic Lands; Cap'n Johne's Hornpipe; Engagement and Malee;
Stones; Greyson's Tale; Fanfare for the Virtuous; The Missing Monarch; Villager
Tarantella; Halls of Doom; Worlds Below; Lord Blackthorn; Dream of Lady Nan;
Joyous Reunion; Rule Britannia; and Amiga Theme.
